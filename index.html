GRADING STANDARDS

Clear grading. No surprises.

Every device is graded against our written standard. We share these definitions upfront so suppliers know exactly what each grade means — no unfair regrading on arrival.

GRADECONDITIONCOSMETIC DESCRIPTIONFUNCTIONAL
A
Like New / Pristine
No visible scratches or dents. Screen flawless. May show extremely faint signs of handling only under close inspection.100% functional. Battery health 85%+. All buttons, cameras, speakers, Touch ID/Face ID working.
B
Good / Very Good
Light scratches on body or screen, visible only under certain lighting. No dents or cracks. Minor wear consistent with normal use.Fully functional. Battery health 80%+. No functional defects. All features tested and working.
C
Fair / Used
Visible scratches, scuffs or minor dents on body and/or screen. Screen may have light scratches that do not affect display. No cracks.Fully functional. Battery health 70%+. May have minor cosmetic issues but all core functions operational.
F
Faulty / Parts
Cracked screen, heavy dents, missing components, or significant cosmetic damage. Sold as-is for parts or repair.May have functional issues — broken screen, non-charging, bad battery, IMEI issues, or other defects. Priced accordingly.
QUALITY CONTROL

Every device. Every batch.

Our 4-step quality control process ensures every shipment matches the agreed grade. We verify before you ship, and again on arrival.

1

Stock List Review

We review your detailed stock list — model, quantity, grade, IMEI status and known issues — before any commitment.

2

Sample Verification

For new suppliers, we request sample units or detailed photo/video evidence to confirm grading matches our standard.

3

Pre-shipment QC

Optional third-party inspection (SGS / Intertek) or our own QC team can verify batches before container loading.

4

Arrival Reconciliation

On arrival, we reconcile the shipment against the agreed list. Any discrepancies are documented and resolved fairly.
